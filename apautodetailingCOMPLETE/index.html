What We Do

Detailing, Engineered For Perfection

Every service is performed by hand with professional-grade products and process discipline — the same standards a high-end showroom demands, brought directly to you.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing that removes swirl marks, oxidation, and fine scratches to restore true depth and clarity. Available as a refining one-step enhancement or a full two-step correction.

Ceramic Coatings

A durable, hydrophobic nano-ceramic layer that chemically bonds to your paint — delivering years of gloss, UV protection, and effortless cleaning. Coating packages are tailored to your vehicle.

Wax & Paint Protection

Premium carnauba and synthetic sealants that amplify shine and shield your finish from the elements — included with every exterior detail for a slick, mirror-like surface.

Carpet & Seat Extraction

Hot-water extraction that lifts embedded dirt, stains, and odors from carpets and cloth seats — pulling contaminants out of the fibers rather than just masking them.

Full Interior & Exterior

Our signature complete detail — a thorough exterior hand wash and protection paired with a deep interior clean, leaving your vehicle refreshed from wheels to headliner.

Paint Decontamination

Clay-bar and chemical decon that strip bonded fallout, road grime, and industrial contaminants from the clear coat — the essential foundation before polishing or coating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to provide at my home or workplace?

Just one thing: a working water hose hookup within reach of the vehicle. We carry our own generator, so you don't need to provide power. Everything else — products, tools, towels, water hose, machines — arrives with us.

How long does a detail take?

A standard exterior or interior detail typically runs 1–2 hours. A full interior and exterior package is usually 2–4 hours, and paint correction or ceramic coating work can take most of a day depending on the vehicle's size and condition. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

Where do you offer service?

We're based in Kernersville, NC and serve the surrounding Triad area — including Winston-Salem, Greensboro, High Point, Oak Ridge, and Walkertown. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask — 336-402-7336.

How does pricing work?

The rates on this page are honest starting points. Final pricing depends on vehicle size (a coupe and a three-row SUV are different jobs) and condition (pet hair, heavy staining, or years of swirl marks take more time). You'll always receive a firm quote before any work begins — no surprises.

What's the difference between wax, sealant, and ceramic coating?

They're all protection with different lifespans. Wax delivers warm gloss for weeks; synthetic sealants protect for months; a professional ceramic coating chemically bonds to the paint and protects for years, with unmatched gloss and easy washing. We'll recommend the right level for how you use your vehicle.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

Not the whole time. Most clients meet us for a quick walk-around at the start, then get on with their day while we work. We'll walk the finished vehicle together at the end so you can see every detail up close.
